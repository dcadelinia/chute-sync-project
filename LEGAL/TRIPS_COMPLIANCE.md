# WTO TRIPS Agreement Compliance Matrix
## International Intellectual Property Protection Strategy

This architecture asserts international trade secret standing under **Section 7, Article 39 of the WTO TRIPS Agreement** (Protection of Undisclosed Information).

### 1. Requirements for Global Enforcement
To maintain cross-border enforceability under TRIPS, the system components verify:
* **Secrecy:** The specific algorithms, weight calculations, and 3D spatial intelligence frameworks are not generally known or readily accessible to the public.
* **Commercial Value:** The code derives distinct commercial value precisely because it remains secret (e.g., maximizing the monetization of the Corporate Alliance Division).
* **Reasonable Steps:** Secrecy is guarded via hardware-level encryption (`src/security/encryption.py`), restricted repository access, and mandatory non-disclosure policies.

### 2. International Dispute Jurisdiction
Any cross-border piracy or industrial espionage will be pursued under the domestic trade secret frameworks of the respective WTO member state (e.g., using the Defend Trade Secrets Act in the US or respective national execution rules).
